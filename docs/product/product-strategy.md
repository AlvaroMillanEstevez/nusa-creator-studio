# Product strategy

## MVP strategy

Build the full workflow platform with mock providers first. The goal is to validate product flows, credit mechanics, project history, media uploads, admin controls and creator UX before committing to real external AI or payment integrations.

## Current mock tools

- Affiliate Campaign Generator
- Video Enhancer / Caption Pack
- Product-to-Video Script Pack
- AI Voiceover
- Fashion Try-On
- Motion AI Video

## Why mocks exist before real providers

Mock providers let the team prove the platform architecture without paid API costs, provider lock-in or fragile early dependencies. They make it possible to test async jobs, credit charging, media requirements, generation history and UI result rendering using deterministic outputs.

## Beta examples and proof strategy

- In beta, Nusa can show a carousel of generated examples.
- Examples should come from internal tests or beta users.
- Each example should identify the workflow used, such as Affiliate Campaign Generator, AI Voiceover, Fashion Try-On, or Motion AI Video.
- Do not imply commercial results unless those results were measured and can be verified.
- Demo and mock outputs should be labeled clearly so users understand what they are seeing.

Possible future proof types:

- Real user examples with permission.
- Anonymized beta workflows.
- Measured time saved.
- Measured content variations created.
- Real testimonials.
- Real case studies.

## Suggested future provider integration order

1. AI Voiceover real provider
2. Fashion Try-On real provider
3. Motion AI Video real provider
4. Payment gateway

## Future cost and pricing research

Before enabling paid providers or checkout, research provider unit economics, expected creator usage, local payment behavior, refund/support needs and Indonesia/SEA payment gateway options. Pricing should reflect real generation costs, platform margin and simple creator-facing credit bundles.
