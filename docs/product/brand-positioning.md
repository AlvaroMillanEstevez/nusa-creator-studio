# Brand positioning

## Product name

Nusa Creator Studio

## Category

AI content studio for social commerce creators.

## Initial positioning statement

An AI content studio for social commerce creators. Create product scripts, captions, voiceovers, try-on concepts and motion video plans from one workspace.

## Core audience

- TikTok affiliates
- Social commerce creators
- Online sellers
- Small brands
- Creator teams

## Core promise

Turn product ideas and assets into creator-ready content workflows.

## Core workflows

- Product scripts
- Captions
- Campaign ideas
- Voiceovers
- Fashion try-on concepts
- Motion video plans

## Brand tone

Practical, modern, creator-friendly, Asia-first but global-ready.

## Future brand architecture

Nusa Creator Studio is the parent platform. Future sub-products may exist later, for example: NusaClip by Nusa Creator Studio.
