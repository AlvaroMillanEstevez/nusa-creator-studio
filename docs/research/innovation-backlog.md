## Future AI automation ideas

- Auto-generate weekly TikTok affiliate content plans
- Auto-localize top-performing scripts into multiple languages
- Auto-create hook variants from performance data
- Auto-match product assets to the best content format
- Auto-suggest when to create voiceover, try-on, or motion video assets
- Auto-detect risky claims or compliance issues before export
