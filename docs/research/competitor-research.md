# Competitor Research

Nusa Creator Studio is an AI content studio for social commerce creators. This document is a research template; use TODO markers until details are verified from current sources.

## Research goals

- Map products used for product scripts, captions, voiceovers, try-on concepts, and short-form video planning.
- Compare pricing, packaging, credits, and usage limits after external verification.
- Identify gaps where Nusa can be simpler, mobile-first, localized, and workflow-focused.

## Landing page inspiration notes

Competitors such as Creatify may inspire the idea of showing product examples, output galleries, demo workflows, and carousel sections. This inspiration should stay at the level of communication structure and product storytelling, not copied claims.

Nusa should not copy exaggerated claims or unverified metrics. Do not invent fake customer logos, generated revenue, ad spend, user counts, testimonials, or case studies.

For now, Nusa should use:

- Demo generation examples.
- Beta examples.
- Before/after workflow screenshots.
- Product-to-output examples.
- Clearly marked mock outputs.

Future real metrics may be added only after they are true and verifiable.

## Opportunities for Nusa Creator Studio

- Build around social commerce jobs-to-be-done instead of generic content generation.
- Keep the workflow mobile-first for creators operating from phones.
- Make internal credits easy to understand before adding real payments.
- Connect scripts, captions, voiceovers, try-on concepts, motion plans, and media in one workspace.
- Prioritize SEA creator workflows: TikTok affiliate, Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, WhatsApp selling, and multilingual content.

## Open questions

- Which competitors are strongest in Indonesia and SEA?
- Which products are truly mobile-first?
- Which pricing models feel easiest for creators to understand?
- Which competitors expose APIs suitable for future integrations?
- Which features are table stakes versus differentiators?
